Checking your app version

Open Android Settings → Apps → Ambient Screen → App details, or Settings → Apps → See all apps → Ambient Screen, to see the installed version.

Common questions

The app doesn't ask for any permissions - is that normal?

Yes. Ambient Screen plays only the video scenes bundled inside the app itself, so it never asks for camera, storage, location, or similar permissions. It does use the internet in the background to send crash reports and anonymous usage statistics - see the Privacy Policy.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. All scenes are bundled with the app and play without a connection. When you are online, the app sends crash reports and usage statistics as described in the Privacy Policy.

How do I set a sleep timer?

Open any scene, tap the screen once to show the controls, then drag the timer slider at the bottom to 30 min, 1 hour, 3 hours, or Unlimited.

How do I switch scenes while one is playing?

Swipe left or right anywhere on the screen, or tap to show the controls and pick a scene from the row at the bottom.

How do I go back to the scene list?

Use your device's back button or back gesture at any time.
