Privacy Policy (English)

This policy describes how the Ambient Screen app (package com.micutsoft.AmbientScreenDisplay, developed by Micutsoft) handles information.

1. Information we collect

Ambient Screen has no sign-up, login, or input forms. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or precise location.

Starting with app version 1.0.2, the app uses the services below to improve stability and usability. They collect the following information automatically:

ServiceProviderInformation collectedPurpose
Firebase CrashlyticsGoogleCrash stack traces, app state at the time of a crash, device information (model, OS version, etc.), a Crashlytics installation identifierFinding and fixing crashes
Google Analytics for FirebaseGoogleApp instance ID, app usage events (app opens, screen views, sessions), device information, approximate location (country/region) derived from IP addressUsage statistics
Microsoft ClarityMicrosoftTaps and swipes, screen layout, device type/brand/model, OS and version, screen size, app name/version, app errors, country/regionUnderstanding how the app is used and improving screens
  • We do not collect the advertising ID. The app has no ads; the advertising ID permission is removed and Firebase's advertising ID collection is turned off.
  • Microsoft Clarity does not take screenshots or video of your screen, and it automatically masks personal information.
  • The app stores a few values on your device only, to decide when to show Google Play's review prompt (first launch time, number of completed viewing sessions, time and app version of the last prompt).

2. How information is used and shared

  • Information is used only to fix crashes, improve stability, and analyze usage.
  • It is processed by the providers above (Google, Microsoft) on our behalf. We do not sell it or share it with third parties for advertising.
  • All transfers use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Video playback works without an internet connection. When offline, this information is not sent, or may be sent the next time the device is online.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Retention follows each provider's policy (Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days by default; Google Analytics follows the project's retention setting; Clarity follows Microsoft's policy).
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all app data stored on your device.
  • To request deletion of information already sent, email us and we will submit the request to each provider.

4. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
